# Delete your data

_Last updated October 28, 2026._

You can delete what FiveStar knows about you yourself, in the app, at any time. If you would rather we do it, or you
want your whole account deleted, email us (see [Ask us to delete everything](#ask-us-to-delete-everything)).

## Forget everything FiveStar remembers

1. Open [askfivestar.ai](https://askfivestar.ai) and sign in with the account you used before.
2. Open **What FiveStar remembers** (the memory button at the top of the page).
3. Tap **Forget everything about me** at the bottom of the panel.

This erases your memory, interests, preferences and trips, and the progress of any email reading. It also
disconnects Gmail and revokes FiveStar's access at Google. The conversation on the page is cleared too.

To remove a single memory instead, tap the remove button next to it in the same panel. To delete a single trip,
open **Your trips**.

If you used FiveStar without signing in, do the same in the same browser: what FiveStar remembered for you is tied
to that browser.

## Delete your check-ins

1. Open your account (your name or picture at the top right).
2. Open **Your check-ins**.
3. Delete any check-in, or tap **Delete all my check-ins**.

Places you added or corrected stay on the map for other travelers, with your name removed.

## Delete a review

Open the place, find **Your review**, and tap **Delete**. Your name, picture and words are removed from it.

## Disconnect each provider

**Google (sign-in, Gmail, Google Calendar).** In FiveStar, open your account and tap **Disconnect Gmail** (and
disconnect Google Calendar if you connected it). This deletes FiveStar's access token and revokes it at Google. You
can also remove FiveStar at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).

**Microsoft (sign-in, Outlook mail and calendar).** In FiveStar, open your account and disconnect Outlook. Then remove
FiveStar at [account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access](https://account.microsoft.com/privacy/app-access) for a
personal account, or ask your administrator for a work or school account.

**Amazon (sign-in).** Remove FiveStar under **Login with Amazon** at
[amazon.com/ap/adam](https://www.amazon.com/ap/adam).

**X (sign-in).** Remove FiveStar at
[x.com/settings/connected_apps](https://x.com/settings/connected_apps).

**Facebook (sign-in).** Remove FiveStar in Facebook under **Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites**
([facebook.com/settings?tab=applications](https://www.facebook.com/settings?tab=applications)). Removing the app
there stops FiveStar from receiving anything more from Facebook; to delete what FiveStar already has, use the steps
on this page or email us.

**Forwarding address and calendar links.** In your account in FiveStar, turn off your forwarding address and remove
any calendar subscription link.

**Linked sign-ins.** In your account, under **Linked sign-ins**, tap **Remove** next to any sign-in you no longer
want linked.

## Ask us to delete everything

Email [support@lucenia.io](mailto:support@lucenia.io) from the email address on your FiveStar account (or tell us
which sign-in you use), with the subject "Delete my FiveStar data". We will delete your account and everything
linked to it: memory, interests, preferences, trips, check-ins, reviews, linked sign-ins and connections, and revoke
any email or calendar access. Places you added or corrected stay, without your name. We confirm by email when it is
done, within 30 days.
